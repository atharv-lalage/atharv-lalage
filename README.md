### 🚧 Featured Projects

**🛡️ DeepShield** — `PyTorch` `FastAPI` `React.js` `LLaMA 3.3` `MTCNN` `ViT`
A deepfake detection system processing image, video, and audio inputs — combining MTCNN, Vision Transformers, and SigLIP for multimedia manipulation detection, with LLaMA 3.3 (via Groq API) generating explainable AI reports.

**📈 TradeX** — `Node.js` `Express.js` `React.js` `MongoDB` `Socket.io` `JWT`
A full-stack stock trading platform with live market tracking and portfolio management. Integrated the Yahoo Finance API over Socket.io WebSockets for real-time price streaming (no HTTP polling), with JWT auth, bcrypt hashing, and Joi-validated REST APIs.

**📊 DataMart** — `Python` `Flask` `React.js` `PostgreSQL` `Pandas` `Scikit-learn` `Groq API`
An AI-powered Business Intelligence platform with ETL pipelines, OLTP/OLAP processing, and a Snowflake-schema data warehouse. Interactive dashboards for customer segmentation, sales forecasting, and market-basket analysis, plus Groq-LLM-generated business insights.

**🤖 NoteBot** — `Python` `Streamlit` `LangChain` `Google Gemini` `NumPy`
A RAG-powered study assistant for PDF-based Q&A, AI summaries, and flashcards — using vector embeddings, cosine similarity, and LangChain document chunking for accurate semantic retrieval.
